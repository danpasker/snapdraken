## The brief

Translate an established visual world into durable, buildable scenery. The specific park and show package are intentionally held back. The case study focuses on the work itself: scale, finish control, field fit, and a clean handoff.

## From sample to full scale

Every finish began with a physical sample. Color, sheen, texture depth, and edge treatment were settled in a piece small enough to compare under show light. The approved recipe then became a practical shop document, including materials, application order, and touch-up notes.

Full-size forms were carved, coated, sanded, and painted with the viewing distance in mind. Close surfaces carried finer breaks and quieter color shifts. Overhead pieces used broader cuts so their silhouette stayed clear from the path below.

## Field finish

Installation never ends at the last bolt. Seams were closed, adjacent trades were blended into the scene, and wear points received a final protective system. The finished work read as one environment rather than a set of shop-built components.
