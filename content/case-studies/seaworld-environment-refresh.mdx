## The brief

Refresh an operating guest environment without treating the existing work as disposable. The first task was diagnosis: determine what needed structural repair, what needed a new coating system, and what only needed a disciplined scenic repaint.

## Repair before color

Loose coatings were removed and damaged substrate was opened far enough to make a sound repair. Foam, hard-coat, fiberglass, and sealants were matched to the condition and exposure of each area. Only then did the paint work begin.

Existing color was recorded before cleaning and compared again after the substrate stabilized. New scenic layers rebuilt depth without making the repaired areas look newly patched. High-touch and weather-facing surfaces received finish systems selected for their actual use.

## Phased return

Work zones were planned around access, cure time, and the daily operating schedule. Completed areas were documented with material notes and color references so later maintenance could start from a known recipe instead of another round of guesswork.
