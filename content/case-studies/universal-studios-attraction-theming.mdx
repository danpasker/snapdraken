## The brief

Extend an attraction story into the physical surfaces a guest meets at arm's length. The project remains unnamed here, but the working standard is clear: the environment has to read quickly, reward a closer look, and survive continuous public use.

## Material language

Profiles began as carved foam and machined parts, then received the hard-coat, fiberglass, or resin system appropriate to their location. Scenic paint was built in layers rather than applied as a single color. Deep values established the form; dry brush and glaze brought back the wear, edge, and age.

Transitions received the same attention as hero elements. Joints, access panels, and changes in substrate were incorporated into the visual language so maintenance did not interrupt the story.

## Installation

Shop assemblies were indexed before shipping and documented for the field crew. The install sequence protected completed finishes while leaving room for the mechanical and electrical trades. Final blending happened on site, where sightlines and show lighting could be judged at full scale.
