## The brief

Build at the scale of a game arena and keep every surface legible on camera. The work had to move quickly from approved drawings into shop-ready parts, then arrive on site in an install sequence the production crew could actually use.

## The shop plan

Large forms were broken into repeatable assemblies sized for machining, finishing, transport, and repair. Scenic surfaces were considered alongside structure from the first pass. That kept the visible work clean without turning install day into a puzzle.

The fabrication plan accounted for crew access, camera angles, resets, and the ordinary abuse that comes with a working set. Nothing depended on a fragile finish or a single difficult connection.

## On site

The final handoff was a built environment, not a collection of props. Each unit had a place in the load-in order. Touch-up materials and repair notes traveled with the set. The result was ready for the production schedule as soon as the last connection was made.
