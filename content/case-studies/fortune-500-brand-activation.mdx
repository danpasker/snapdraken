## The brief

Turn a tightly controlled brand system into a place people could enter, use, and photograph. Across the selected builds, the practical requirements stayed consistent: every environment had to travel, fit a defined freight plan, install on schedule, and stay legible across a crowded show floor.

## One build, many rooms

Each environment was divided into modular frames, skins, and show pieces with positive indexing at every connection. Graphic zones were planned around seam locations. High-wear components could be removed and replaced without dismantling the full installation.

Finishes were tested under the lighting conditions expected on site. Color stayed within the approved range while surface texture did enough work to keep large areas from reading as flat print.

## Pack and install

Every piece had a crate position and an install number. Hardware traveled with the component it served. The field book covered order of work, adjustment points, touch-up materials, and the strike sequence. The installation could move without losing the finish standards set in the shop.
