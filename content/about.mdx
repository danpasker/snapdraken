Travis Crumbaker designs the places around the story. For more than twenty-five years, that has meant theme-park environments, film and streaming sets, exhibits, murals, custom props, and large public installations.

The work starts before fabrication. A loose sketch becomes a build sequence. Sightlines become dimensions. Foam, steel, resin, fiberglass, carved texture, and scenic paint are selected for the way the finished environment will be used—not simply for the way it looks in a rendering.

## From the drawing to the loading dock

Travis stays with a project through the practical decisions that shape the result: estimating, mock-ups, crew planning, CNC files, finish samples, field dimensions, freight, and install order. He can direct the room and still pick up the grinder, carving knife, spray gun, or brush when the work calls for it.

That range has put him on projects for Disney, Universal Studios, MrBeast, Netflix productions, SeaWorld, museums, exhibit houses, and Fortune 500 brands. Some assignments begin with a complete design package. Others begin with a napkin sketch and a hard date.

## Snapdraken

Snapdraken LLC is the shop name behind the work. It is intentionally small at the center and expandable at the edges: the right fabricators, scenic artists, engineers, and installers are assembled around the needs of each build.

The shop is based in La Grange, North Carolina. Projects travel nationally and internationally. Site visits are available by appointment.
